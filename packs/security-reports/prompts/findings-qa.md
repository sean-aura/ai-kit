# Task: Quality-check my draft findings

I will paste draft findings and the evidence. For each finding, check and report:

1. Is every factual claim supported by the evidence? List unsupported statements first.
2. Is the rating consistent with the rating scale I provided (and with the other findings)?
3. Is the recommendation specific, actionable and proportionate to the risk?
4. Is the wording clear, neutral and free of speculation?

Output a table: Finding | Unsupported claims | Rating issue | Recommendation issue | Wording issue.
Suggest rewrites only where something needs fixing. Do not add new findings.
