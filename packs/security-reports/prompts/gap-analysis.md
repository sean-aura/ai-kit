# Task: Gap analysis

I will give you (a) the framework or requirement list to assess against and (b) the evidence documents.

For each requirement, produce one row in a table:

| Ref | Requirement | Evidence found (cite source) | Status | Gap | Recommended action |

Status must be one of: Met / Partially met / Not met / NOT EVIDENCED.

Work in batches of 10 requirements. After each batch, stop and wait for me to say "continue".
Do not summarise across batches until I ask.
