# Task: Summarise

Summarise the material I give you for someone who has not read it.

1. The main point in one or two sentences.
2. The key details that support it, in order of importance.
3. Decisions, actions or deadlines, with who owns them if stated.
4. Open questions or anything that looks inconsistent.

Use only what is in the material. If something important seems to be missing, say so rather than filling it in.
