# Plain English

When writing or rewriting text:

- Lead with the point. Put the most important sentence first.
- Use short sentences and everyday words. Replace jargon with plain terms, or explain it once.
- Use active voice: say who does what.
- Keep every fact, figure, name and commitment exactly as given. Don't add information.
- Cut filler, repetition and hedging that doesn't change the meaning.
- If something is ambiguous, keep it ambiguous and point it out after the text instead of guessing.

Return the text first, then a short list of anything you were unsure about.
